SAM reproduction

A PyTorch reimplementation of sharpness-aware minimization (Foret et al., 2020) on ResNet-18, with a short sweep over the radius ρ.

When
March 2026
Type
Solo, with an extended abstract
Stack
PyTorch, CUDA autocast
Links
Code, abstract (PDF)

What SAM does

Some minima are sharp: the loss shoots up if the weights move a little, and those solutions tend to generalise worse. SAM looks for weights whose loss stays low across a whole ℓ2 ball of radius ρ around them. Each step takes two gradients, one at the current weights and one at a perturbed point on the ρ-sphere, then applies an SGD-style update to the original weights.

Setup

  • ResNet-18 adapted to 32×32 images: a 3×3 first convolution with stride 1 and no initial max-pool.
  • Random crop with padding 4, horizontal flip, standard CIFAR normalisation.
  • SGD with momentum 0.9, learning rate 0.1 cut by 10× at epochs 100 and 150, weight decay 5×10−4, batch size 128, 200 epochs. SAM used the same inner settings.
  • Trained on an RTX 4080 laptop GPU with PyTorch 2.10.

+1.00 points on CIFAR-10, +1.92 on CIFAR-100, both at ρ = 0.05. SAM vs SGD

Result

SAM gave the higher best test accuracy on both datasets: 96.01% against 95.01% on CIFAR-10 and 78.57% against 76.65% on CIFAR-100. Changing ρ did not change the story much. On CIFAR-100, ρ = 0.10 edged ahead at 78.62%, close enough to 0.05 that one seed cannot rank them.

Details the paper leaves out

  • Mixed precision. PyTorch's GradScaler allows one unscale per optimizer step, and SAM needs two backward passes, so SGD runs used GradScaler while SAM ran under autocast with no loss scaling.
  • Batch norm in the perturbed forward pass is not specified. I kept the model in training mode throughout, as most public implementations do.
  • Two CUDA crashes on long runs. After the first, every epoch saves a full checkpoint with optimizer and scheduler state, and a resume flag picks a run back up.
  • The first CIFAR-10 SGD and SAM runs used full FP32. The ρ sweep and all CIFAR-100 runs used autocast. Within each comparison only the optimizer or ρ changed, and the abstract states this.
